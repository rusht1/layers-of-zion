# Layers of Zion

An original 2D browser platformer about how Zion National Park formed. Players climb up through Zion's rock layers, from the Moenkopi tidal flats to the carving of the canyon, collecting field notes that explain how each layer formed. A final boss level quizzes players on what they learned.

**Play it:** https://rusht1.github.io/layers-of-zion/

Made by Ryan Rushton for Geology 101. The research and field notes are my own. The game was built with help from Claude.

## Levels

| Level | Rock layer | Approximate age |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Tidal Flats | Moenkopi Formation | 250–240 million years ago |
| 2. River of Logs | Chinle Formation | 225–210 million years ago |
| 3. Track Country | Moenave and Kayenta Formations | 210–185 million years ago |
| 4. Sea of Sand | Navajo Sandstone | 185–180 million years ago |
| 5. Return of the Sea | Temple Cap and Carmel Formations | 175–165 million years ago |
| 6. Carving the Canyon | Erosion by running water | The past 1 million years |
| Final level | The Strata Golem | All of the above |

## Controls

Arrow keys to move, Space to jump, X to swing the rock hammer (or throw geodes), Down to dig, N for the field notebook, M to mute, P to pause. On a phone, hold it sideways and use the on-screen buttons.
